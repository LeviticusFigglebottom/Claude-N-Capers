# Walkthrough detour whitelist for Ostrander Federal (tools/levelcheck/README.md "walkthrough").
# Format: <spawn name|path|*> <target name|path|*>. Owner: ENV C (OF-C1, G1). Every entry is a > 3x path/straight
# ratio that follows from the APPROVED plan (docs/design/heists/ostrander_federal.md, D-075 / D-136; the floor plans in
# tools/levelcheck/plans/ostrander_federal_floors_ascii.py), not from a navigation fault: every target is reachable
# (validate_level `reachability` PASS) and the path is the shortest legal walk. The bank has three street entries and
# all of them are on two faces: the bronze doors (x 22-26) and the ATM vestibule's street doors (x 3-5) on the plaza
# (south) face, the dock personnel door (x 7.4-8.4; the roller is baked closed, D-073) on Ledger Lane (north). The east
# face (Tannery St) has no entry (the BC fire exit is push-bar outward, locked from outside), and the west face is
# party-wall to the Corvino block. Every level change is Stair A (x 14-15.5, z 0-7) or Stair B (x 46-47.5) in the north
# block (the cash lift carries bags, not crew; the hole and the hoist are loud-only and disabled at start; the fire
# escape and the caged ladder are masked-only ladder links to the roof and the terrace). So every room above or below
# the ground floor, and every ground room on the east face, is a walk round to a door and back along a corridor while
# the straight line passes through a facade, a slab or the hall floor. Entries list every spawn of a spawn group that
# fails for each target (regenerated from validate_level's walkthrough FAILs, baseline variants + the slot targets).

#

# straight line down through the hall floor (3-5x). The gallery-level east rooms (OF3, TR, TT) are Stair A / B -> UC
# -> EL; the terrace escape (E3) is Stair A -> XC -> XL -> the terrace doors (x 5-11, z 17) -> the terrace (E level,
# over the hall roof).
